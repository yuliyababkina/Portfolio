# App design

Status: approved 2026-11-25

## Product
Personal portfolio website of Yuliya Babkina, a Senior Product Designer, aimed at senior to Lead-level UX and product design roles. Planned structure: a one-page home plus case study pages (to confirm).

Used by hiring managers, design leaders, recruiters and peers. They skim fast, so the site must read as confident, calm and senior within seconds, and make the work easy to reach.

## Rules
- **R1** Minimalistic, stylish and user-friendly: clean layouts, generous whitespace, nothing decorative. (2026-11-25)
- **R2** Monochrome with one accent colour. The accent is used sparingly, only to draw attention. (2026-11-25)
- **R3** Geist is the typeface. (2026-11-25)
- **R4** Bold typography: large, confident headings carry the design. (2026-11-25)
- **R5** shadcn components are the base. Library first: compose from the catalog and keep the build clean. (2026-11-25)
- **R6** Icons only when needed, and only from Lucide. (2026-11-25)
